# 01 · Brief & Business Understanding

Source: `BFouru_Company_Brief_and_Services.docx` (supplied by the client).

## The business in one paragraph

BFourU is a full-service **creative, digital, technology and business-support agency**. It combines brand identity, design, marketing, media production, websites/apps, automation, IT support, print, signage, events and merchandise under one roof. **Healthcare is a specialist vertical** (doctors, clinics, hospitals, diagnostic centres, medical colleges, associations), **not the identity of the whole agency**. This is the single most important constraint on positioning and design.

## What BFourU sells (14 service lines → 5 clusters)

The brief lists 14 service lines with ~160 individual deliverables. Showing 14 equal cards (as most competitors do) creates choice overload. We regrouped them into **five clusters** based on the *kind of problem* a customer is trying to solve:

| Cluster | Services | Customer's mental model |
|---|---|---|
| **01 Brand & Creative** | Branding & Identity · Creative Design · Photography & Media Production | "How we look and sound" |
| **02 Digital & Web** | Digital Marketing · Website & App Development | "How people find us online" |
| **03 Technology & Automation** | Smart Automation · Clinic Management Software *(proposed)* · OPD TV & Digital Displays · IT & Technical Solutions | "How our operations run" |
| **04 Spaces & Print** | Interior Branding & Signage · Printing & Production · Customized Merchandise | "How we show up physically" |
| **05 Events & Education** | Events & Conferences · Patient Education Solutions | "How we communicate at key moments" |

Every service keeps its own detail page (SEO + depth), but the clusters drive navigation.

## Business objectives (inferred from the brief)

1. **Generate qualified enquiries** → custom quotations (no public price list; "pricing is provided through customized quotations").
2. **Make breadth feel like strength, not like a generalist**. The value proposition is "one point of contact for multiple creative and digital needs".
3. **Win healthcare trust** with ethics-aware positioning (the brief repeatedly stresses consent, doctor-approved content, advertising ethics and no diagnosis by chatbots).
4. **Support two engagement models**: project-based and subscription-based.

## Hard constraints & honesty rules adopted for the website

- **No fabricated proof.** The brief contains no client list, testimonials, case studies, awards or statistics. The site therefore uses *no fake logos, numbers or testimonials*. Trust is built from clarity, process, ethics commitments, and visible craft. Slots are designed so real proof can be added later (see `CONTENT-TO-CONFIRM.md`).
- **Clinic Management Software is "Proposed"** and is labelled as such everywhere, with the brief's caveat.
- **Exclusions are shown openly** (no medical equipment, medicines, staffing, or clinical services).
- **Healthcare disclaimers**: BFourU does not provide medical care, clinical content requires professional approval, and patient imagery/data needs consent.
- **Contact details** (phone/WhatsApp `+91 91829 70829`, email) are marked in the brief as "pending confirmation", so they live in a single config file (`src/data/site.ts`) for a one-line change.

## Reading of the name

"BFourU" reads naturally as **"B-for-U" / "Be for you"**. We use this lightly in the brand voice ("built around you", "for you") but do not claim it as an official meaning. Confirm with the client before using it explicitly.
