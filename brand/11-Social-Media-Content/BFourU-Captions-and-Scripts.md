>
> 1. Clinical content is approved by the treating doctor

## Engagement routine (15 minutes a day)
- Reply to every comment and DM within the working day, in the same channel.
- Never discuss a patient's condition in comments or DMs. Move it to a private, appropriate channel at the clinic.
- Don't buy followers, likes or reviews, and don't run giveaways that ask for reviews.
