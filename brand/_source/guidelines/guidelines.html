How to use this guide

A system, not a set of rules to memorise.

Everything here is built from a small number of decisions. Learn those, and most design questions answer themselves.

For designers

Start with the foundations (sections 02–06). Use the templates in /07-Templates rather than rebuilding layouts.

For developers

Use /05-Design-Tokens. Colours, type, spacing, radius and motion are named tokens — never hard-code values.

For marketing teams

Use the social and presentation templates and the voice guide (section 07). Check healthcare rules before publishing.

For agencies & printers

Read sections 02, 03 and 11. Request a physical colour proof before every print run.

02
Logo.

A modular mark and a confident wordmark — one system, many outputs.

Primary logo · Full colour reversed on Forest Ink
Anatomy

Four cells. One is always for you.

The mark is a 2 × 2 modular grid built from a quarter-round, a circle, a square and a quarter-round. The Persimmon circle is the part that is specifically for the client. The four cells also nod to the “Four” in BFourU.

  • Grid — 40 × 40 units; cells 18 units; gap 4 units
  • Wordmark — Bricolage Grotesque Bold, tracking −35, outlined
  • Proportion — mark 0.92 S · gap 0.36 S · wordmark 0.8 S (S = logo unit)
  • Alignment — wordmark cap height centred on the mark

Never re-type the wordmark. Always use the supplied artwork.

Placement

Corners, not centres.

The logo anchors a composition from the top-left or bottom-left margin. Align it to the same margin as the text it sits with.

Voice.

Plain, specific, warm and honest — like a senior consultant who listens.

Tone of voice

Four principles.

Plain

Short sentences, everyday words. If a client wouldn’t say it, we don’t write it.

Say

“We automate the reminders.”

Avoid

“We leverage omnichannel automation synergies.”

Specific

Name the deliverable, the audience, the outcome.

Say

“Prescription pads, signage and a website with booking.”

Avoid

“End-to-end 360° solutions.”

Warm

Speak to “you”. Be kind and direct.

Say

“Tell us what you have today. We’ll suggest what’s next.”

Avoid

“Kindly revert at the earliest.”

Honest

No superlatives, no guarantees, no invented numbers.

Say

Principles & timing

Quick feedback, calm reveals.

Purposeful

Calm

BFourU Brand guidelines08 Motion

Motion in use

One behaviour per element.

Logo animation

The four cells rotate in from −90° and scale 40→100%, staggered 70 ms; the Persimmon circle lands last. Total ≤ 1.2 s. The wordmark fades in after.

Typography reveals

Headline lines rise from 105% inside a mask, 1100 ms, stagger 110 ms. Body copy fades up 14 px after 350 ms.

Page transitions

Fade + 10 px rise, 600 ms, only on in-app navigation — never on first load.

Scroll animations

Reveal once at 8% visibility: 28 px rise + fade, 700–900 ms. No scroll-jacking, no parallax on text.

Hover interactions

Buttons: Persimmon fills from below (420 ms) and the arrow swaps. Links: underline draws left→right (380 ms). Cards lift 3 px.

Presentations

Morph between slides (1.25 s) with shared “!!” object names; on-click reveals using Fade, Float In, Wipe and Zoom only.

Social motion

6–10 s loops; each line holds ≥ 1.5 s; logo end-card 1.5 s; captions burned in; keep text in the centre 80% safe area.

3D motion

Not part of the current identity. If used: one slow rotation ≤ 15°, matte brand colours, never behind text.

Avoid

Bounce, elastic, spin, flash, typewriter effects, confetti, auto-playing sound, motion longer than 1.5 s for UI.

Print production

Getting it right on paper.

Bleed & safe area

3 mm bleed on all edge-to-edge items; keep text 5 mm inside trim (7 mm on business cards). Letterhead and envelope are designed without bleed for office printing.

Colour

Supplied PDFs are RGB with embedded fonts. Ask the printer to convert to their CMYK profile and supply a hard proof. Approve Persimmon and Forest Ink on the actual stock.

Resolution & files

Images 300 ppi at final size (150 ppi for large-format viewed from > 2 m). Logos always vector (SVG/PDF). Never place PNG logos in print.

Paper

Uncoated warm white or natural stocks suit the brand. Business cards 350–400 gsm; brochures 170 gsm text, 300 gsm cover.

Finishes

Optional: blind emboss of the symbol, Persimmon edge paint on cards. Avoid gloss lamination and foil.

Large format

Standees and signage are supplied at 1:1 vector size. Confirm the hardware’s visible area and base height with the vendor.
